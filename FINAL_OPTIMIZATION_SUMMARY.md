## ✅ OPTIMIZATIONS IMPLEMENTED

### 1. API Caching Strategy ✅
**File:** `src/utils/serverApi.js`

**Changes:**
- Changed from `cache: 'no-store'` to `cache: 'force-cache'` with revalidation
- Banner data: Revalidates every 1 hour (3600s)
- Homepage content: Revalidates every 1 hour (3600s)
- SEO data: Revalidates every 2 hours (7200s)

**Impact:**
- ✅ Reduces server load
- ✅ Improves response times
- ✅ Better TBT and Speed Index
- ✅ Content stays fresh with revalidation

---

### 2. Image Optimization - Added `sizes` Attribute ✅
**Files:** 
- `src/app/components/Card.jsx`
- `src/app/components/RowImage.jsx`
- `src/app/home/page.jsx`

**Changes:**
- Added `sizes` attribute to all Image components
- Banner images: `sizes="100vw"`
- Card images: `sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, (max-width: 1200px) 50vw, 33vw"`
- Row images: `sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, 50vw"`

**Impact:**
- ✅ Browser loads appropriate image sizes
- ✅ Reduces bandwidth usage
- ✅ Improves LCP
- ✅ Faster image loading on mobile
